Hershey vector fonts: public-domain glyph data, in James Hurt's .jhf form
=========================================================================

futural.jhf is "Hershey Sans" (a single-stroke sans), one of the Hershey vector
fonts drawn by Dr. A. V. Hershey at the U.S. Naval Weapons Laboratory in
Dahlgren, Virginia, around 1967, and published by the U.S. National Bureau of
Standards in 1976. The glyph data is in the public domain.

This file is the ".jhf" per-face form (the format James Hurt created), with one
glyph record per printable ASCII character. It is bundled as the default for
Ollin's StrokeFont (single-line / plotter text) and loaded at runtime by the
.jhf parser.

The .jhf distribution may be used by anyone for any purpose, commercial or
otherwise, provided that these acknowledgements are distributed with the font
data, and that the data is not converted into the format distributed by the
U.S. NTIS. The acknowledgements, as the distribution words them:
