Hershey vector fonts — public domain
=====================================

futural.jhf is "Hershey Sans" (a single-stroke sans), one of the Hershey vector
fonts originally created by Dr. A. V. Hershey while working at the U.S. National
Bureau of Standards. The Hershey fonts are in the public domain.

This file is the ".jhf" (James Hershey Font) per-face form, with one glyph record
per printable ASCII character. It is bundled as the default for Ollin's
StrokeFont (single-line / plotter text) and loaded at runtime by the .jhf parser.

Provenance:
  - Author: A. V. Hershey / U.S. National Bureau of Standards
  - Distribution: the widely mirrored public-domain Hershey data
    (e.g. https://github.com/kamalmostafa/hershey-fonts and
     https://paulbourke.net/dataformats/hershey/)
  - Status: public domain (no license required)

The public domain imposes no obligations; this notice records provenance for the
bundled asset, consistent with how Ollin documents everything it ships.
