## Finished piece against what the chapter teaches

The bench composes code-written maps, triplanar stone, a worn ball (four maps), a parallax tile, a subdivided star in `.brushedMetal`, a loaded model as glass on `.lacquer`, felt, marble wax, a decal, one environment. It omits `loadScene`, from-scene, `wireframe`, wrap modes, 3D text, Hopf, booleans, `MeshGrowth`, occlusion and emissive maps, sky, feed, thin film, transmission. The normal-map encoding appears only here (649 to 666). Errors: 684 and 717 call the crystal the model "the chapter opened with" (line 26 loaded a duck); "cushion" names both the star (9, 717) and the felt (730); variation 899 asks for the sheen the piece has.
