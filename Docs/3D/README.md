## 3D

- [`3D`](./3D.md) - opt into a 3D camera and a depth buffer. You orbit a `Camera3D` (perspective or orthographic) and draw `PointCloud`s as instanced disc splats. You can also draw a catalog of solid primitives (box, sphere, capsule, the Platonic solids, …) and parametric and profile shapes (supershape, extrude, lathe). Meshes load from file (`.obj`, `.usdz`/`.stl`/`.ply`, `.gltf`/`.glb`). Textured surfaces and a live webcam depth cloud draw too.
- [`Scenes`](./Scenes.md) - `loadScene` keeps a glTF file's *structure* instead of merging it. You get a tree of named nodes, drawn in place with `drawScene` and reached by name to animate (`scene["lamp"]`). The scene also gives you the authored camera and its punctual lights as ready `Camera3D`/`Light` values.
- [`Instanced meshes`](./Instancing.md) - draw one mesh thousands of times in one call. `drawMesh(_:instances:)` takes a list of `MeshInstance` placements (position, rotation, scale, tint), and the GPU applies one to each copy. It can take a `ComputeBuffer` instead, which a kernel writes, so a simulation moves the copies without the CPU. The copies shade like solid meshes and cast into the shadow maps. A retained `MeshField` scales this to a whole world drawn by one call, with each copy GPU-culled against the camera.
- [`The Hopf fibration`](./HopfFibration.md) - a sphere's worth of circles, where no two circles meet and every two are linked exactly once. `hopfFibers` returns them as `[Vector3]` paths for `drawTube`. `hopfBases` supplies the structured base sets (rings of latitude, or a spiral) that make the linking easy to see.
- [`Strand fields`](./Strands.md) - grass that a mesh pipeline grows inside the draw call itself. `StrandField` + `drawStrands` draw half a million bending, swaying blades with no vertex or instance buffer anywhere. The blades are tile-culled by the camera and graded in detail by distance, and they are shaded and receive shadows on the solid lit path.
- [`The ocean`](./Ocean.md) - a sea built the way the real ocean is measured. `oceanField` writes a wave spectrum, and one inverse Fourier transform turns it into the moving surface. `drawOcean` draws that surface as water with no geometry anywhere. `waveHeight` is a measurement in world units, not a dial.
- [`Combining 3D features`](./Combining.md) - the practical map of which 3D features work together. It says which geometry takes lights, materials, matcaps, and environment light, and which things cast, receive, and appear in shadows and reflections. It says when to use screen-space reflections and when to use ray-traced ones, and it ends with a step-by-step recipe for the most realistic scene.
- [`Camera control`](./Camera.md) - move the camera by hand or play a ready-made cinematic move. `cameraControl()` gives you drag to orbit, scroll to dolly, and right-drag or modifier-drag to pan, all damped. `cameraMove(_:)` plays a turntable, push-in, tilt, orbit-and-rise, reveal, or handheld move. Both are opt-in, and both reuse the orbit pose.
- [`Caustics`](./Caustics.md) - the light that glass and metal focus. `caustics()` photon-traces the caster light through every transmissive and mirror-polished surface and draws where it lands. As a result, a glass sphere throws its bright spot into its own shadow, and a chrome ring concentrates reflected light beside itself. `dispersion:` fans out a prism's rainbow. `caustics()` needs a ray-tracing GPU.
- [`Lens flare`](./LensFlare.md) - the light the camera adds by itself. `lensFlare()` works out the ghosts that a real lens prescription makes of each bright source. The prescription places and sizes each ghost, the iris blades give it its shape, and the anti-reflective coating gives it its color. It also draws the star on the source itself, and the star has as many arms as the iris has blades. All of it fades as something covers the source, instead of switching off.
- [`Atmosphere`](./Atmosphere.md) - make the air visible. `fog` fades surfaces with distance and pools low with a height falloff, using an exact closed-form transmittance. `aerialPerspective` splits the fade by wavelength and lights it with the sun, so far ridges take on a blue haze and blend into the sky. `volumetricLight` ray-marches through the air, so spot cones, cookies, IES profiles, and cast shadows become visible beams and crepuscular shafts.
- [`Depth compositing`](./DepthCompositing.md) - place 2D drawing *inside* a 3D scene, so it can hide the geometry and the geometry can hide it. The calls are `depth(at:)`, `project`, and `withBillboard`, which hides a 2D label when it swings behind the cloud.
- [`Record3D`](./Record3D.md) - `import OllinRecord3D` to turn an iPhone's color-plus-depth into a 3D point cloud. The source is a recorded `.r3d` file or the live USB stream of a tethered phone.
- [`RGBD`](./RGBD.md) - `RGBDFrame` (color + depth + intrinsics) is the one frame type that every depth source produces. From it you unproject a point cloud, lift a single image point to metric 3D, or lift a 2D body pose into space (`Body.lifted(through:)`).
- [`Phone`](./Phone.md) - `import OllinPhone` to read a tethered iPhone's live ARKit sensor stream over the USB cable. The stream comes from Ollin's own capture app, and ARKit runs on the phone. The stream carries a 3D body skeleton, a face mesh with expression blendshapes, world-facing rear-LiDAR depth, and device motion. The depth arrives as a metric point cloud with the camera's 6DoF pose.
